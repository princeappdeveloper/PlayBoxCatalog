Featured App

For Android

Elixo: Music & Video Player

A local media player for Android. Elixo discovers and plays the music and videos already on your device — no account and no cloud uploads. It is built for the way people actually keep media: organised into folders, easy to browse, and ready to play.

  • Plays local music and video, with full-screen video playback and background music playback.
  • Organises your library into folders, with playlists, favorites and playback history.
  • Recently added, recently played, continue watching and most played views to pick up where you left off.
  • PIN-protected Private Media and a Recycle Bin for safe delete and restore.
  • On-device by default: your media is never uploaded to us.

About

About Fyndora Apps

Fyndora Apps is an independent mobile app brand focused on building practical Android applications with a clear purpose.

Practical by design

We build apps that are easy to understand, quick to start using, and pleasant to return to. Every screen is there to help you do something, not to get in the way.

Details that matter

We care about the fundamentals — responsive performance, sensible defaults, and interfaces that stay readable and comfortable on phones, tablets and desktops.

Privacy-conscious

Wherever possible, our apps work with what is already on your device. Elixo keeps your media on-device, and our published privacy policy explains exactly what each app does.

Built to last

We aim for reliable experiences rather than feature lists. Thoughtful updates, steady maintenance and respect for the people using our apps are what we focus on.
